INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL SERVICES CENTRES AUTHORITY

Circular F.No. IFSCA-FMD/2026/11                              Dated: 3 September 2026

To: All Fund Management Entities registered with the Authority

Sub: Enhanced experience standards for senior risk personnel

1. The Authority has observed a widening of the risk profile of schemes launched from the
   International Financial Services Centre, and considers it necessary to strengthen the
   experience standards applicable to persons heading the risk management function.

2. Accordingly, a Fund Management Entity managing assets in excess of USD 200 million
   shall ensure that the person heading its risk management function, designated as Risk
   Manager, possesses not less than seven years of experience in risk management in the
   financial services sector.

3. Nothing in paragraph 2 shall apply to a Fund Management Entity that manages only
   schemes restricted exclusively to accredited investors.

4. Entities shall attain compliance with paragraph 2 by 1 January 2027. A confirmation of
   compliance, signed by the Principal Officer, shall reach the Authority no later than
   15 October 2026.

5. For the avoidance of doubt, this circular does not alter the experience standards
   applicable to Investment Analysts, Fund Managers or any other personnel.
