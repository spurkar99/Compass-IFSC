INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL SERVICES CENTRES AUTHORITY

Circular F.No. IFSCA-AML/2026/04                                 Dated: 20 July 2026

To: All Regulated Entities

Sub: Frequency of anti-money laundering refresher training

1. Clause 8 of the IFSCA (Anti Money Laundering, Counter-Terrorist Financing and Know Your
   Customer) Guidelines, 2022 requires every Regulated Entity to maintain an ongoing
   employee training programme.

2. With effect from 1 November 2026, and notwithstanding the twelve-month interval
   presently observed, every employee of a Regulated Entity shall undergo an
   anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing refresher programme at intervals
   not exceeding six months.

3. Records of attendance shall be retained for a period of five years and shall be
   produced to the Authority on request.
